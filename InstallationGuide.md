<h1>Install CopyBear</h1>
</div>

Download the latest CopyBear from [releases](https://github.com/Crazelu/copybear/releases) and unzip it, then follow the steps below.

## 1. Move CopyBear to Applications

**Drag `CopyBear.app` into your `Applications` folder using Finder before launching it.**

This step matters: if you launch the app straight from your Downloads folder, macOS runs it from a randomized, read-only location (Gatekeeper "app translocation"). CopyBear will still work, but it won't be able to update itself — you'd have to download every new version manually.

## 2. Allow CopyBear to open

CopyBear is ad-hoc codesigned, so the first time you open it macOS will warn that it could not verify the app is free of malware. To open it anyway:

1. Double-click `CopyBear.app`. When the warning appears, click **Done** (not "Move to Trash").
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and scroll down to the **Security** section.
3. You'll see a message that CopyBear was blocked — click **Open Anyway** and authenticate.

On older macOS versions (Ventura and earlier), you can instead Control-click the app in Finder and choose **Open**. Apple's general guide is [here](https://support.apple.com/en-ng/guide/mac-help/mh40616/mac).

You only need to do this once. After that, CopyBear launches normally.

## 3. Updates are automatic

From version 3.0, CopyBear checks for updates in the background and shows a banner in the app when a new version is available — one click updates in place. You won't need to repeat any of the steps above for updates installed through the app.
